EMBER PACT

An elemental card duel against Cinder, the Ash Warden

Outwit the warden. Spend your energy. Break the pact — or keep it.

How to Play

  • Goal: reduce Cinder to 0 life before yours hits 0. Both start at 30.
  • Each turn your energy refills (more each round, up to 10) and you draw up to 5 cards.
  • Click a card to play it if you can pay its energy cost. Keys 1–5 select a card, Enter ends your turn.
  • Element wheel: 🔥 Ember beats 🍃 Leaf beats ❄️ Frost beats ⛰️ Stone beats 🔥 Ember — +2 damage when your element beats Cinder's last played element.
  • Surge: play 2 cards of the same element in one turn and the second gains +2.
  • Watch the telegraph: Cinder warns you what is coming — Guard against a heavy strike, strike back when Cinder mends.
  • Cards: Strike deals damage · Guard gains shield · Mend heals · Spark draws a card.
